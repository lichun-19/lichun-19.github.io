<p class="highlight-text">
  I envision AI becoming interactive systems that truly augment human capabilities in social intelligence, creativity, cultural nuance, and critical thinking, while helping people understand, reflect on, and diversify human experiences.
</p>

I am an interdisciplinary researcher from [National Taiwan University](https://www.ntu.edu.tw/english/) (NTU), where I currently work with the amazing Professor [Shao-Hua Sun](https://shaohua0116.github.io/). I received my B.S.E. in Electrical Engineering from NTU in July 2025, and was a visiting researcher in Professor [Nanyun Peng](https://violetpeng.github.io/)'s [PLUS Lab](https://violetpeng.github.io/group/) at the [UCLA NLP Group](https://x.com/uclanlp).

I collaborated with Professors Shao-Hua Sun and [Hung-yi Lee](https://speech.ee.ntu.edu.tw/~hylee/index.php) on a series of work about **creativity** and **language models**. I also worked with Professor [Mike Y. Chen](https://mikechen.com/) on **human-computer interaction** research through virtual reality gaming during my undergraduate studies.


⮕ Take a look at some of the [research](https://lichun-19.github.io/publications/) and cool [projects](https://lichun-19.github.io/projects/) I’ve worked on, and get to know [more](https://lichun-19.github.io/more/) about me beyond research!
