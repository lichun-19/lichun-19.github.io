<p class="highlight-text">
  I envision AI becoming interactive systems that truly augment human capabilities in social intelligence, creativity, cultural nuance, and critical thinking, while helping people understand, reflect on, and diversify human experiences.
</p>

My interdisciplinary research explores how AI can better augment human capabilities. At [National Taiwan University](https://www.ntu.edu.tw/english/) (NTU), I work with the amazing Professor [Shao-Hua Sun](https://shaohua0116.github.io/), and I am currently visiting the [Fluid Interfaces Group](https://www.media.mit.edu/groups/fluid-interfaces/overview/) at the [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/).

I received my B.S.E. in Electrical Engineering from NTU in July 2025. During the undergraduate studies, During my undergraduate studies, I worked on a series of research projects at the intersection of **creativity**, **language models**, and  **human-computer interaction**. This included a research visit to Professor [Nanyun Peng](https://violetpeng.github.io/)'s [PLUS Lab](https://violetpeng.github.io/group/) at the [UCLA NLP Group](https://x.com/uclanlp), as well as research presented at leading conferences, including COLM, EACL, CHI, etc.
